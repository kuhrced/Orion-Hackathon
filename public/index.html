About

A party racing game about cleaning up a polluted world.

🏭 The problem

Pollution turns the air grey and makes the planet harder to live on. It's a big problem that can feel far away, so we wanted to make it something you can see and change in a few minutes with your friends.

🏁 The game

The host draws a race track on the big screen. Up to 6 players scan the QR code and race from their phones, steering with two buttons. Add bots if you're short on players.

🌳 Clean it up

The map starts grey and polluted. Answer questions about energy and the planet correctly and the world turns green, lap by lap. Coming soon

🛠️ How it's built

One Node.js server runs the race 60 times a second and sends it over WebSockets. The big screen and the phones draw it with HTML canvas. No app to install: just a browser.
